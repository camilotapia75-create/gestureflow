Quick Wins

5 habits that eliminate
90% of desk pain.

👁️
Screen at eye level

Raise your monitor so your eyes land on the top third of the screen. Eliminates the chin-down tilt that causes tech neck.

🦶
Feet flat, hips at 90°

Both feet on the floor, thighs parallel to the ground. This anchors your pelvis and keeps your spine naturally upright.

⏱️
Stand or move every 45 minutes

Static posture — even perfect posture — fatigues muscles. A 2-minute stand or walk resets circulation and focus.

📱
Raise your phone to eye level

Looking down at your phone adds 60 lbs of force on your neck. Hold it up or rest your elbow on a surface.
